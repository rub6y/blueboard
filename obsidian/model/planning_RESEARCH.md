# planning/RESEARCH.md

`planning/RESEARCH.md` is the research note backing the technical choices in
[[planning_PLAN]].

`planning/RESEARCH.md` covers language choice ([[Rust]] vs C vs Python), the
[[Git_workflow]], the [[File_format]] and [[Compression]] approach, rendering/performance
strategy ([[Viewport_culling]], [[LOD_simplification]], [[Tessellation]] caching), and the
[[Magic_window]] feasibility research ([[Webview_embedding]] vs [[Portal_capture]] vs
window reparenting).

`planning/RESEARCH.md` is accompanied by `planning/math.tex`, the mathematical derivations
for [[Bezier_curve]] fitting/flattening, [[LOD_simplification]], and the [[RTree_index]]
query.
